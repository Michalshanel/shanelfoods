About Us

The story behind Shanel Foods and our mission

Our Story

Started with a Simple Idea

Shanel Foods was born from a simple observation: too many people skip breakfast or settle for unhealthy options because they're too busy in the morning.

As working professionals ourselves, we understood the struggle. Waking up early, rushing to get ready, and often leaving home with nothing but a cup of tea. We knew there had to be a better way.

That's when we decided to create a service that delivers fresh, nutritious breakfasts right to your doorstep every morning. No cooking, no prep, no hassle — just healthy food ready when you wake up.
