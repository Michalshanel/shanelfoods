About Shanel Foods

A local Chennai business built on a simple belief — everyone deserves a fresh, healthy start to their morning

Who We Are

Bringing Fresh Breakfasts to Your Door

Shanel Foods started with a simple question: why do so many people skip breakfast or settle for something unhealthy? The answer was usually the same — too busy, too tired, or just not sure what to eat.

We set out to solve that problem by delivering fresh, nutritious, ready-to-eat breakfast boxes directly to homes in Kodungaiyur, Madhavaram, Kolathur, Perambur and Vyasarpadi every morning, Monday to Saturday.

Every box is prepared fresh with seasonal fruits, vegetables, and proteins — no preservatives, no shortcuts. Just real food, delivered with care.
