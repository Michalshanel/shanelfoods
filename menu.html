FAQ

Good to know

How do I order or enquire?

Just message us on WhatsApp with the product you like — or use the enquiry form — and we'll reply with availability, plans and delivery details.

When do you deliver?

Every morning, Monday to Saturday. Sundays are our day off.

Do you have weekly or monthly plans?

Yes! Every product is available as a one-time order, or on weekly and monthly plans. Ask us on WhatsApp for plan details.

Can I switch boxes or pause a day?

Of course. Let us know on WhatsApp at least 24 hours in advance and we'll switch or pause your delivery.

Can you customise for allergies or diets?

We can make adjustments for allergies and dietary needs. Mention it in your enquiry and we'll take care of it.
